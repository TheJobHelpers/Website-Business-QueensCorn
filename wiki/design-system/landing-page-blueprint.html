The brief

The 5-second test

A first-time visitor on a phone must be able to answer, without scrolling:

  1. What is it? Hand-popped kettle corn from Arizona.
  2. How do I get it? Shipped, picked up at a market, or for my group.
  3. When can I get it? The next market, with date and place.

Actions, in priority order

1

Order onlineShipping or free market pickup. Needs Shopify checkout working.

2

Come to a marketThe next pop-up, shown in the hero.

3

Order for a groupFundraisers (50%) and event bookings. Fewer customers, bigger orders.

Page rules

  • Every section ends in one clear action.
  • One red button and one yellow highlight per screen.
  • Real photos and confirmed claims only.
  • Everything visible without waiting for scroll animations.
  • Phone first, fluid up to 2560px+: the hero fills the screen at any size; text, plate and spacing scale with width and height.

Goal

Pass the 5-second test. Say what it is, how to get it, and when the next market is.

Primary action

Order now (red) → /shop. Secondary: Find us this weekend → #markets.

Copy (final)

  • Badge: “Family-run in Arizona since 2017”
  • H1: “Hand-popped kettle corn, fit for royalty.” (the logo's own tagline)
  • Sub: “Sweet, salty and still warm from the kettle. Order online and pick it up free at the stand, or have it shipped to your door.”
  • Flavor card: “In the photo · Caramel & Cheddar · from $6 · + Add” (adds a Small, opens the bag)
  • Pill: “Next pop-up · {day, date} · {market}, {town}” + Pre-order
  • Perks: Free pickup at every market · Free AZ shipping over $35 · From $6 a bag

Data

  • Next pop-up = first upcoming event with pickupAvailable, from getEvents() / events store. Hide the pill if none.
  • $35 from FREE_AZ_SHIPPING_THRESHOLD; “from $6” from the lowest product price.

Design

  • Left: the promise on today’s espresso-brown shade (the stand photo dimmed to an atmosphere).
  • Right: the food. A round “plate” close-up of real caramel & cheddar on a warm kettle glow, a tilted snapshot of Reina at the kettle, a red “Hand-popped in Arizona” sticker, and a floating card to add the pictured flavor in one tap.
  • Cream serif headline with the tagline in yellow italic. No “scroll to explore”, no flavor pills.

Mobile (375px)

  • Order: badge, H1, sub, full-width buttons, pop-up pill, perks, then the food visual (max 330px) above the trust strip.
  • The buttons sit above the fold on a 375×667 screen.

Done when

On a 375×667 screen, the H1, sub, both buttons and the pop-up pill are visible without scrolling; the pill shows the real next market (hidden if none); Lighthouse accessibility ≥ 95.
