01 · Direction

Keep the warmth. Add the brand. Fix the clarity.

The current site already feels warm and premium, and customers and the owners like it. What it lacked was the brand's own color and a page that sells clearly. v3.1 keeps the cream, the serif and the soft cards, and borrows the stand's red and canopy yellow as accents: one red action per view, one yellow highlight per view, real photos everywhere.

Keep from today's site

  • Warm buttercream background and white cards
  • Instrument Serif headlines with Outfit body text
  • Crimson red for actions, espresso for dark bands
  • The dark espresso-brown shade over the hero photo
  • Warm, deep shadows and rounded corners
  • Real photography of Bob, Reina and the stand

Change

  • Kettle Gold → Canopy Yellow from the tent, used as one highlight per view
  • Muted text darkened to pass contrast (was 4.6:1, now 5.9:1)
  • Sage and copper retired, except as flavor coding
  • Fake reviews, unverified claims and AI-generated photos removed
  • Content hidden behind fade-in animations made visible at rest

03 · Typography

The serif you know, used with more confidence

Instrument Serif stays for headlines, prices and big numbers. It's set larger than before, with a red italic for the one phrase that matters. Outfit stays for everything people read and tap. Two families. The script lives only in the logo.

08 · Voice

Talk like Bob & Reina at the stand

Friendly, proud, a bit playful, never corporate. “We” is Bob & Reina; “you” is the customer. Short sentences. Facts beat adjectives.

Say

“Hand-popped kettle corn, fit for royalty.”

“The Queen's Corn is a happy place.”

“Corn oil, sugar and salt. That's it.”

“Pre-order and pick it up warm on Saturday.”

Don't say

“Elevate your snacking journey” · “Premium experiences” · “Artisanal excellence”

Unverified numbers or rankings (“#1”, “$34,000 donated”) until confirmed

Emoji in headings or buttons · ALL-CAPS sentences · stacked exclamation marks

09 · Rules for changes

How to change the look without breaking it

Every UI change (people & AI agents)

  1. Use tokens from tokens.css: no raw hex, ad-hoc font sizes or one-off shadows.
  2. Reuse a component from section 06 before inventing one.
  3. One red button and one yellow highlight per view.
  4. Check contrast against the approved pairs.
  5. Real photos and confirmed claims only.
  6. Check at 375px and 1440px.

Changing the system itself

  1. Edit tokens.css, components.css and this page first.
  2. Mirror into src/app/globals.css in the same commit.
  3. Record why in wiki/decisions/ and a line in wiki/log.md.
  4. Bump the version here and in tokens.css.
