Shop page blueprint · v1 · 2026-10-10 · Design System v3.1 “Warm Canopy”

A shop where you get your bag in three taps

Every shopper answers the same three questions: how do I get it, which flavor, which size. The page asks them in that order, on one screen, and keeps the bag total in view. The mockup below uses the real tokens and components.

Design system Homepage blueprint Build plan

What's wrong today

  • A tall “Flavor Vault” banner pushes the flavors below the fold.
  • No way to pick a size on the card; “Quick add” silently adds a Family bag.
  • Pickup vs shipping only shows up after you chose it somewhere else.
  • On phones the filter hides in a dropdown.
  • Fade-in animations and an AI-image promo card at the bottom.

The three taps

  1. How do you want it? Ship to me, or pick up at a market (choose which).
  2. Which flavor? Filter chips by taste, with counts.
  3. Which size? $6 / $10 / $15 right on the card, then “Add”.

Rules

  • Flavors visible on the first screen, on phones too.
  • Every card shows all three sizes and prices.
  • Bag total + free-shipping progress always in view once something is added.
  • Real photos only; a clear placeholder where a bag photo is missing.

Chips

One row of taste chips with counts and the flavor color dot; scrolls sideways on phones (no dropdown). Sticky under the nav while scrolling the grid.

Card

  • Photo with the taste tag on it; name; one line; one taste meter.
  • Size picker on the card ($6 / $10 / $15, Small preselected), quantity stepper, and “Add · $price” showing the line total.
  • Tap the photo or name for the full product page.

Bag bar

Appears once the bag has items: count, total, free-shipping progress (or “Free pickup at {market}”), and the one red button. Sticky at the bottom on phones.

Mobile

2 cards per row; description hidden; sizes stay (the point of the page). Bag bar pinned to the bottom with safe-area padding.

Done when

From a cold load on a phone, a shopper can choose pickup, filter “Spicy”, pick “Family”, add 2 and reach checkout without leaving the page or scrolling back up. Real bag photos needed for 7 of 8 flavors
